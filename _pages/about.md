I am an Assistant Professor in the Division of Social Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. 

My research examines how social factors shape stress-related psychological distress and biological aging. In particular, I am interested in how people interpret and make meaning of their social experiences, and how those interpretations promote resilience—or vulnerability—to stress.

I use population-based surveys, biomarkers (including epigenetic measures of biological aging), social network analysis, and qualitative methods to study the relationship between social experiences and health.

In my free time, I enjoy playing sports, watching movies, and spending time with my collaborator (see Collaborator page). 

Research Interests
======
- Stress, trauma, and resilience
- Mental health (particularly depression, anxiety, PTSD, and substance use)
- Social genomics
- Biological aging
- Social networks
- Health inequality
- Gender
